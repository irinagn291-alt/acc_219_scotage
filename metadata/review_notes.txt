Three behaviours are deliberate.

1. Save lot stays unavailable until Lot name has text and Amount is above zero. Fill both, leave Period on Monthly, set Next charge to a day in this month, then tap Save lot. Cancel closes the form and writes nothing.

2. A lot whose Next charge falls in another month is kept, but it does not ring this month's rim. The face can still read No lots this month. Set Next charge to a date in the current month so the lot bills now.

3. When the month total sits at or under the Scot pin, the job line reads Load sits inside the Scot. Add a lot or lower the pin. The hand does not aim. A tap on a lot shows Aim first. Then tap the lot. Open Settings and lower Scot pin, or add a larger lot, until the load passes the pin.
